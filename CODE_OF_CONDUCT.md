# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Criticise ideas and code, not the person
who wrote them.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming, particularly to people who are new to the project or to the
  problem space.
- Accept that reasonable people disagree about design, and that being right is
  less useful than being understood.
- Give feedback on the work rather than the author.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, and move on without drama.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled
privately. Responses range from a request to stop, through to a permanent ban,
depending on severity and whether the behaviour continues.

This is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
